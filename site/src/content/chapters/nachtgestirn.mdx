The Moon as a renderable body rather than a point in the sky. Where it is, how large it appears, how it is tilted and
lit, and how much of Earth's own light reaches its night side all come from [sternzeit](../sternzeit/): its phase and
sun direction, its libration and position angle, its earthshine, and whether Earth's shadow is falling on it. What is
left is the look: a textured disc, shaded by the sunlight on its surface, ashen in earthshine, and during a lunar
eclipse darkened and reddened by the sunlight Earth's atmosphere bends into its umbra. Its glow in turn lights the night
sky of [dunstkreis](../dunstkreis/).
